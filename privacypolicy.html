Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 9, 2026
Organization: Country View Estates Water System

Country View Estates Water System ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy details how we handle the collection, use, and security of information obtained when you opt in to receive SMS text message notifications regarding our water system operations.

1. Information We Collect

When you subscribe to receive water service notifications, we collect the following personal details:

  • Contact Information: Your full name and mobile phone number.
  • Consent and System Metadata: Proof of your explicit opt-in consent, including registration timestamps, IP address at the time of submission, and the exact disclosure text agreed to.

2. Purpose of Information Use

Your mobile phone number and personal information are collected and used exclusively for:

  • Sending essential text notifications regarding water service disruptions, system pressure drops, emergency repairs, planned maintenance schedules, boil water advisories, and water quality alerts.
  • Maintaining verifiable consent records in accordance with carrier guidelines and telecommunications regulations (such as CTIA standards and the Telephone Consumer Protection Act).
  • Responding to incoming support inquiries triggered by your mobile device (e.g., automated response when you text HELP).
Strict Third-Party Sharing Prohibition:
Country View Estates Water System does not sell, rent, lease, or trade mobile phone numbers or personal data. Your mobile information and SMS consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes under any circumstances. Data is shared solely with vetted communications service providers exclusively to transmit text notifications on our behalf.

3. Message Frequency and Rates

  • Message Frequency: Notification frequency varies depending on water system operations, active maintenance, and unforeseen emergency repairs.
  • Carrier Charges: Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and wireless plan. Country View Estates Water System does not charge an additional fee for text notifications.

4. How to Opt Out or Get Assistance

You may control your subscription status at any time:

  • To Stop Receiving Alerts: Reply STOP, END, CANCEL, or UNSUBSCRIBE to any text message from Country View Estates Water System. You will receive a single final confirmation text verifying your removal, and no additional messages will be sent unless you re-register.
  • Customer Support: Reply HELP to any message for assistance instructions, or contact water system management directly.

5. Data Security & Retention

We maintain appropriate technical and administrative safeguards to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. Opt-in records and phone numbers are securely retained for as long as you maintain active subscription status or as required to demonstrate legal compliance.

6. Contact Information

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our messaging practices, please reach out to the management of Country View Estates Water System.
